# CivilCvCAD Privacy Policy

CivilCvCAD’s built-in application features do not collect, transmit, or share
personal data. The distributed application disables network-backed workbenches,
downloads, remote debugging, online help, external website commands, Qt
networking paths, and embedded-Python internet sockets.

CivilCvCAD stores configuration, logs, recovery data, thumbnails, caches, and
recent-file information on local storage. CAD documents and macros can contain
metadata and local paths.

An operating-system firewall is still recommended for strict containment.
Arbitrary native plugins or separately launched external programs run outside
CivilCvCAD’s embedded-Python and Qt safeguards and must be controlled by the
operating system.
